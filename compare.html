Onionz searches on your phone, not on a server

Onion search engines run your search on their own servers. Onionz builds the index on your phone, so your searches never leave it.

What Onionz does not do

Onionz starts with an empty index, and building one over Tor takes time and battery. It does not filter what it finds, so what you crawl and open is your responsibility. It is a test build, for Android only, not on Google Play and not independently audited.
